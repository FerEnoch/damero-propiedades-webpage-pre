Never set text in sage. Its 3.42:1 on surface fails normal-text AA. Use sage-ink (6.72:1), including for labels, placeholders, disabled text and text inside SVGs. Sage is for non-text graphics only.

On tint bands, use forest borders for interactive controls. Sage on tint is 2.84:1: below even the non-text minimum. Sage control borders are valid on surface or canvas only.

Anti-patterns

Source: docs/DESIGN.md §11; sage prohibition from §2 and §12. This is an archive, not a marketplace template.

First and absolute: sage is never rendered text. Not even headings, placeholders, disabled labels or SVG text. Use sage-ink for meaning carried through type.

Portal density and urgency
No dense 4-up grids, saturated badges, DESTACADO ribbons, photo corner tags, fake urgency, scarcity or social proof.
Photo overlays and price colours
No text, badges, prices or gradients over photographs. No red/green prices; use text-primary only.
Competing actions and spreadsheet filters
No more than one primary per viewport. No spreadsheet-like filter bar or saturated pill filter chips.
Heavy shadows and glow
No shadow on a resting card. No neon, outer glow or coloured-glow focus ring. Elevation tint is forest, never neutral black.
Stock clichés or missing media
No smiling agents, handed-over keys, generic skylines, broken or hotlinked stock images. Only §7's downloaded, committed provisional-photo exception applies before launch.
Fabricated data or advice
No invented counts, years, testimonials, response times, credentials, registration numbers or legal statements. Unknown required values stay PENDIENTE.
Generic type and emoji
No emoji, Inter, or generic production serifs: Times, Georgia, Garamond, Palatino. This offline board's system fallbacks are an explicit artifact exception.
Off-palette colour
No pure black, neon or oversaturated accents, gradient headings, or colours outside §2. White is surface/on-forest only, never the page background.
Glassmorphism
No blur, translucent panels or backdrop effects. Header is a sticky opaque surface with a hairline.
Pills, circles and oversized icons
No 999px radii, pill buttons/chips, circular icon containers or a large rounded icon above every heading.
Template grouping
No three-equal-card feature row, nested cards, or cards used purely to group content.
Centred text and unnecessary modals
No centred heroes or text blocks. Use inline/full-page patterns; the mobile filter sheet is the single approved modal exception.
Decorative interaction
No custom cursor, “Scroll to explore”, “Swipe down”, bouncing chevrons, scroll arrows or perpetual animation.
Filler formatting and copy
No LABEL // YEAR. No “Elevá tu experiencia”, “Encontrá el hogar de tus sueños”, “Soluciones sinérgicas”, “Next-Gen” or “Seamless”.
Off-brand WhatsApp fill
§6: WhatsApp brand green is banned as a fill. Use forest primary styling; identity comes from the decorative glyph and real label.
Meaning by hue alone
§12: use text, fill weight, border and shape. Every action has a visible 2px sage-ink focus ring with a 2px offset; targets are at least 44×44px.
