Negociación — three ways to draw an agreement

The brief is fixed: the copy is Negociación — "Te acompañamos en todo el proceso para lograr las mejores condiciones." and it stays. Only the glyph is in play. All three proposals below hold the duotone contract of docs/DESIGN.md §8 — a 24×24 viewBox, a 1.5px currentColor line, and one solid sage accent mass behind it. Pick one below; the next board covers Asesoramiento legal.

What changed in the search.

The literal two-handed handshake survives at 24px only if the silhouette comes from a professionally drawn form, not from hand-placed strokes. Proposals A and B therefore adopt the Lucide handshake geometry (ISC licence, attribution recorded in the commit) and re-tone it into the house duotone. Proposal C drops the handshake entirely for a metaphor that needs no fingers at all.

Note the set rule in §8: "Do not use the accent mass as the only differentiator between two icons — shape must differ." All three pass — the mass sits in a different place and the silhouette is different in each.

The Lucide clasp with the mass moved onto the palm of the near hand. The palm is the one region that stays a closed shape once the fingers are drawn as line, so the accent reads as anatomy rather than as a sticker. At 24px the two hands are still legible as two hands.

Lucide handshake (ISC) + sage palm mass. 5 strokes, one mass. Reads at 24px.

Negotiation as dialogue: two overlapping speech marks, one filled sage. No fingers, no sub-pixel detail, so it is the most legible of the three at 24px and the cheapest to keep crisp. The trade-off is that it loses the physical 'agreement' reading and sits closer to the FAQ teaser in meaning.
